# sales-architecture

CIO/executive-facing presentations: branded slide decks, architecture briefs, and discovery-to-presentation pipelines for enterprise sales.

## Routing

description: Prompts that generate enterprise sales artifacts — CIO-ready slide decks, discovery briefings, and branded architecture presentations — for MuleSoft/Salesforce sales contexts.
use_when: The audience is a CIO, CTO, or executive buyer. The output is a branded slide deck, POV brief, or sales architecture presentation. The context is an enterprise deal or discovery session.
not_when: The audience is an engineer or architect consuming a technical payload — use systems-architecture instead. The output is an unbranded human summary — use product-delivery instead.

## Prompts in this domain

- `PRM-NBLM-005` — Discovery-to-Architecture Extractor
- `PRM-NBLM-006` — 6-Slide Enterprise CIO Presentation Generator
