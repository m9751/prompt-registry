# product-delivery

Human-facing outputs: executive briefings, slide summaries, meeting recaps, stakeholder reports.

## Routing

description: Prompts that transform technical or data-heavy inputs into clear, human-readable deliverables for non-technical audiences — executives, stakeholders, or clients.
use_when: The output is a briefing, summary, overview, report, or slide deck intended for a human presenter or reader. The consumer is a person, not another system.
not_when: The output is a machine-readable payload (JSON, structured extract, semantic payload) — use ai-engineering or systems-architecture instead. The output is a sales presentation with branded slides — use sales-architecture instead.

## Prompts in this domain

- `PRM-NBLM-001` — Slide-to-Human Briefing Master
- `PRM-PDLV-006` — Static Guide Overview Video
